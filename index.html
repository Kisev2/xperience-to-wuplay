Migrate to Wuplay

To migrate collections directly inside your browser:

  1. Tap and hold the button above, select Copy Link.
  2. Create any bookmark on your phone, name it "Migrate to Wuplay", and paste the code into its URL field.
  3. Open config.wuplay.app on your phone.
  4. Tap your address bar, type "Migrate to Wuplay", and select the bookmark. A neat overlay box will instantly pop up on the page for you to upload your files!
